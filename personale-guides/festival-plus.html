Festival+ Guide

Festival+ is the optional, tiered membership: the guest picks a pass (Spark / Pulse / Power) that bundles a ticket, camping, Beats and VIP perks, and pays with Beats. It is not the same as the required 75 kr Festival Pass. When active, the guest wears a FESTIVAL+ badge.

Guest   Admin

Guest (app) chooses a tier and becomes a member, paying with Beats. Admin (FMS) configures the tiers as products; membership is granted automatically on purchase.

Festival+ vs Festival Pass. Festival Pass = the required 75 kr entry credential (see its guide). Festival+ = an optional membership that includes a ticket + perks and is paid in Beats. Different products, different purpose.

PART 1

Guest: become a member (app)

native app

From the home screen the guest taps Se Festival+, picks a tier, confirms, and pays with Beats. The membership then shows as active with a badge.

  1. On the home or settings screen, tap Se Festival+ (“Become part of the festival”).

  2. Choose your pass: Spark, Pulse or Power. See What you get, then Continue.

  3. Confirm membership: plan, Full season duration, expiry, and payment from Your Beats (with Top up if short). Tap Become a member.

  4. Welcome to {tier}: “Your membership is active.”

    The guest now carries a FESTIVAL+ badge and the tier’s perks (ticket, camping, Beats, VIP).

PART 2

Admin: tiers & grants (FMS)

back-office

  1. The three tiers are products configured for the summit (name, benefits, Beats included, price). The app falls back to Spark/Pulse/Power defaults if none are set.

  2. Membership is granted automatically on purchase. When the guest’s order is confirmed, the subscription entitlement (active + tier + expiry) is created. No manual step per guest.

    The guest’s Status shows Active with an Expires date; the badge appears in the app.
