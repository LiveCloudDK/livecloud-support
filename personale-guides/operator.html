Bemanding, CSV import, access, Beats
& the webticket flows

Step-by-step guides for the operator (admin) tasks in FMS Bemanding and what leaders and staff see on the webticket. Every screen below mirrors the live UI; the actual Danish labels are shown in grey, e.g. Importér, so you click the right thing.

Where you work: the operator side is under Akkreditering → Bemanding in FMS, the whole staffing structure lives here. Leaders and staff work on the webticket (webticket.ringstedfestival.dk). Make sure the correct summit is selected first (top-left, Ringsted Festival).

  1. Role (Roller) = Områdeleder · Teamleder · Medarbejder. Team (Område) = the team name, identical for the whole team.

Choose fileImportér
  1. Pick the sheet (Choose file) and click Importér. Areas, teams, leaders and staff slots are created, with fee/Beats/shifts/access set from the columns.
  2. Warnings appear if an access column matches no ticket product (it is skipped), or a row is missing area/e-mail.

Guide 3The structure

The import builds a hierarchy: festival → area → team → staff. Leaders become owners of their unit.

Guide 6Invitations & QR

People with e-mail get a mail; teams without e-mail get a QR to self-enrol.

Send invitationer
2Del tilmeldings-QR

One QR/link per team, for people without e-mail.

Del tilmeldings-QR
  1. Send invitationer mails everyone invited who has an e-mail and resends to non-responders.
  2. Del tilmeldings-QR per area/team: people scan and enrol themselves, even without e-mail.
What they see: the person lands on the webticket, signs in with MitID / e-mail code, and claims their slot.

Guide 7Move & close access

  1. Flyt next to the person → pick a new team. The person and their status move with them.
  2. Luk adgang withdraws the person's access (e.g. on a no-show). The action is logged.
  3. A claimed person's data survives re-import; only open slots are rebuilt.

Guide 8Access & SKUs

Access columns are issued as tickets. On shift for several days → the access is issued per day.

Setup (one-time, per summit)
For each access, create a ticket product (SKU) whose handle carries the zone + day token, e.g. …-backstage-torsdag-…. The per-summit access map connects the column name to the zone token.

  2. Day multiplication: two shift days → two tickets for that access. Camping ticks work the same way.
  3. If the SKU does not exist yet, the import warns you, so you can create it before the festival.

Guide 9Export to Danløn

  1. Click Eksportér → the file holds name, fee, CPR + address for løn staff.
  2. Fictitious test CPRs (from MitID test) are deliberately blanked with a warning, so test data can never reach real payroll.
  3. Missing CPR/address on a løn person shows as grey pills, remind the person to complete their profile.

Guide 13Hand out access

  1. Sæt navn on a slot (or name only, no e-mail, becomes a slot to fill later).
  2. Bulk QR: one QR/link for several slots at once.
  3. Print passes (QR + name + day). The access becomes a scannable ticket on their profile.

Guide 15Invitations & contracts

  1. Gensend an invitation to a non-responder, or invite via QR so people enrol to your team themselves.
  2. Under Kontrakter you can view contracts and sign your own digitally; staff sign theirs on their own login.
